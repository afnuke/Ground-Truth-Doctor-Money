# Ground Truth: Doctor Money

**What the doctor is paid to prescribe.**

An app in the Ground Truth series. Drug and device companies must report every payment they make to doctors into a federal database — the CMS Open Payments program, under the Physician Payments Sunshine Act. Doctor Money shows those reported payments plainly: the record the companies themselves filed.

## The seed data

- **Greenville, SC — program year 2024:** 1,855 physicians with a Greenville address received **$3,714,110.41** in general payments (24,863 payment records) from 511 companies.
- Every figure comes from the official CMS Open Payments datasets — the only source. No third-party estimates, nothing invented; where CMS has no value, the app shows none.

## The standing caveat

Receiving payments from drug and device companies is legal, and these payments are reported under federal law. **A payment listed here is not evidence of wrongdoing.** The app presents the record; what it means is for the reader to judge.

## How it works

See what a physician received, from which companies, and for what — consulting and speaking fees, royalties, meals, travel, education — as categorized by the reporting company. No ads, no accounts, no selling your data.
